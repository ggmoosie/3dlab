Cortlandt Station

Layout v3, "The Arcade". The hub level for LootNShoot, drawn to scale from plan.py, the one source of truth the Blender layout and the Unreal build both read. Three levels: the city street, a bright underground arcade (the safe hub), and the island platform where the train leaves for raids.

MMezzanine · the safe hub84 × 24 m, z 5.2, clear 3.6 m
  • Flow. Street stairs land in the Gate, past the watch post and the turnstiles into the Market, then the Node.
  • The Node holds the main stair down, the raid board on the north wall and a compass-rose floor. Trade post, infirmary, gunsmith and stash are the four bays that touch it.
  • Glass shopfronts both sides of an 8 m concourse, white riveted columns, four glass display kiosks down the centre line.
  • Bunks beside the stash: wake, gear up, reach the raid board in about 15 s.
PPlatform · the way out70 × 9 m island, z 0
  • Track A (south): the live train, 4 cars. Board between the two stair feet; pick a stop at the route board by the lead cab.
  • Track B (north): 3 derelict cars, then the test range down the tunnel with targets at 10, 25 and 50 m.
  • I-beam columns every 15 ft in two rows; both tunnel mouths fade to black to hide the ride loading screen.
1Street · danger zone and tutorial200 × 124 m, z 10
  • Tutorial path (dashed blue): overrun NYPD checkpoint, Cortlandt St, Cortlandt Drugs for the first loot, the green-globe stair, down. About 60 m.
  • Entrances are ordinary sidewalk stairs against the building line: green globes open, red globes welded shut.
  • Barricade ring (red dashes) with 12 zombie spawners outside; the Tide breaks in at telegraphed gaps.
  • 84 storefronts, 18 enterable, 3 walk-ups; a FEMA tent camp in the pocket plaza.

Decisions before modelling

Shop bay widthBays are 12 m wide (8 m deep). Four players fit at the trader counter with about 1 m each. Keep 12 m, or go to 14 m and shorten the outer bays?
Black market accessBehind the NO ENTRY staff door. Locked until a quest unlocks it, or always open?
Red entranceThe south (red globe) stair is welded shut as a landmark. Should it ever open (event, late-game shortcut), or stay shut?
Street interiors18 enterable shops out of 84 fronts. Build all 18 in the first pass, or 6 hero interiors first (drugstore, bodega, deli, pawn shop, laundromat, walk-up lobby)?
Test rangeDown the Track B tunnel at 10 / 25 / 50 m, reusing the GunRange target kit. OK to share that kit, or a station-themed target set?

Build order after approval

  • Textures + print atlas: subway tile, green trim, terrazzo, I-beam paint, brick, asphalt, sidewalk; signage and route board on one atlas.
  • Kit in Blender: columns, shopfront module, kiosk, turnstile, stair + railing, globe newel lamp, train car, platform edge, street furniture. Gate: mesh_qc 0 errors.
  • Layout from plan.py into map.blend + layout.json (meshes, shell boxes, spawners, lights, doors); Cycles review sheet.
  • Export FBX per kit piece + the UE import and map-build scripts (run on your machine: this cloud box has no Unreal).
